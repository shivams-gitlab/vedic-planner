Hora Vedic Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: Sep, 2026

Hora Vedic Planner ("the app", "we", "us") is a Jyotish (Vedic astrology) planning tool that shows horā periods, pañcāṅga data, and lets you optionally schedule your own events and sync them with your Google Calendar. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

1. Information we access

The app can access the following, only with your explicit action:

  • Location you enter-a city or coordinates you search for, used solely to calculate sunrise/sunset and horā timings for that place.
  • Events you create in the app-title, description, time, and color you choose to schedule.
  • Google Calendar events-only if you click "Connect Calendar" and grant permission. See Section 2 for exactly what this covers.

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier to use the core features of the app.

2. Google Calendar integration

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app requests the calendar.events OAuth scope. This scope permits the app to read and create/modify events on your Google Calendar-it does not grant access to your other Google data (Gmail, Drive, Contacts, etc.), and it does not permit changing your calendar's settings or sharing/deleting the calendar itself.

This access is used only to:

  • Create events on your calendar for astrological items you choose to schedule in the app;
  • Read your existing Google Calendar events so the app can display them alongside your horā schedule.
No server-side storage of your Google data. The app is client-side only. Your Google access token is held in your browser's memory for the duration of your session and is never sent to or stored on any server we operate. We do not have a backend database of your Google Calendar data. When you close the tab or your session expires, the token is gone; you simply reconnect next time you want to sync.

You can revoke the app's access to your Google Calendar at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or by using the "Disconnect" option inside the app.

Hora Vedic Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Local data storage

Events you create, your chart-style preference, your default location, and similar settings are stored using your browser's local storage-on your own device only. This data is never transmitted to any server we control. Clearing your browser data, or using a different browser or device, will remove it.

4. Astrological calculations

Pañcāṅga and planetary-position calculations are performed using ephemeris data and, for some calculations, a request to our calculation service (Supabase Edge Functions) containing only the date, time, and location needed to compute the result-not any personal identifier.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third parties.
  • We do not use your Google Calendar data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI/ML models.
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to us from Supportpage.
